# Unify Homepage Brand Styling

## Goal
Refine every homepage section after the hero so the experience consistently reflects Imbuto of Hope International’s navy, teal, and sun-gold identity through the footer.

## Changes
- Rework Who We Are into a calmer image-led introduction with navy typography, teal structure, and restrained gold highlights.
- Restyle Programs with consistent branded accents, clearer card hierarchy, and balanced section contrast.
- Rebuild Impact around the existing photography and counters using a legible navy overlay and only the three brand colors.
- Refine the closing call-to-action and footer transition so buttons, icons, headings, and supporting details feel continuous.
- Remove unrelated pink, purple, green, orange, and raw color treatments from these homepage sections.
- Preserve all current homepage content, links, imagery, counters, and functionality while simplifying distracting decoration.

## Quality Check
- Review the full homepage in light and dark modes.
- Verify desktop and phone layouts, contrast, image loading, links, animations, console output, and runtime behavior.

## Technical Details
- Use the existing semantic color tokens and shared Button component.
- Keep motion restrained and compatible with reduced-motion settings.
- Replace remote imagery with existing project assets where needed.
